# Third-party material

The models in `models/` (`grand-piano-salamander`, `grand-piano-salamander-release`,
`grand-piano-salamander-pedal`) are spectral analyses (partial envelopes, noise spectra and short
attack excerpts) of the recordings of the following sample set, and are distributed under its
license.

## Salamander Grand Piano V3
**Alexander Holm** — Yamaha C5 grand piano, 16 velocity layers sampled in minor thirds, release
and pedal noises; recorded at 48 kHz / 24 bit with two AKG C414 in an AB pair. Licensed under
**Creative Commons Attribution 3.0** (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Distributed
by FreePats: https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Piano/acoustic-grand-piano.html

Changes: the recordings were analysed into spectral models and are resynthesised, not played
back; the release and pedal recordings are taken at the levels the sample set's own SFZ mapping
plays them; the top key's recordings (which sound near C#8) are played as C8.
